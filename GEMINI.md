# NEPSE Trading Desk for Gemini CLI

You have the StockYan tools (NEPSE market data, company filings, broker activity, scanners, the strategy backtester and
the user's StockYan portfolio and paper account). For any question about NEPSE stocks, trades, brokers or the user's
portfolio, follow the NEPSE Trading Desk in `skills/nepse-trading-desk/SKILL.md` and read the station playbooks it
points to in `skills/nepse-trading-desk/references/`.

The short version: call `start_research_session` first; every number comes from a StockYan tool with its date; read the
market regime, find ideas with several strategy sleeves, score them with four analysts, check broker flow, argue the
pros and cons, plan entry, stop and size, check portfolio fit and risk, backtest the setup, and write a plain-language
report with visuals. It is research, not investment advice, and paper trades happen only when the user says yes.
