# StockYan for Gemini CLI

You have the StockYan tools (NEPSE market data, company filings, broker activity, scanners and the user's StockYan
portfolio). For any question about NEPSE stocks, sectors, brokers or the user's portfolio, follow the research method
in `skills/stockyan-research/SKILL.md` and read the playbook it points to in `skills/stockyan-research/references/`.

The short version: every number comes from a StockYan tool call made now, with its date; look at fundamentals,
technicals, broker flow and context; write a bull case and a bear case that rebuts it; give a conditional verdict with
levels to watch; end with sources and one line saying this is research, not investment advice.
