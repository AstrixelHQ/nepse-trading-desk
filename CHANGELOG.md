# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-19)

- First release of **NEPSE Trading Desk**: an eleven-station research desk (market regime, seven strategy sleeves plus
  events, analyst team with a confluence score, smart-money tracker, pros and cons, trade plan with NEPSE gap and
  liquidity caps, portfolio construction, risk desk with veto, paper execution, backtest, plain-language report).
- Works through the StockYan connector, which also serves the playbooks (`get_desk_playbook`), so Claude, ChatGPT and
  Gemini apps get the desk by adding the connector alone.
- Installs: Claude Code plugin (one command), Gemini CLI extension, `npx skills add`.
